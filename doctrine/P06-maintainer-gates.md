# P06: Agents propose, the maintainer ratifies

## Statement

Irreversible or authority-bearing acts pass through an explicit maintainer gate: merging, pushing, adopting or amending law, final verdicts, and process changes. Agents prepare the act completely (the PR, the amendment text, the graded package with a recommendation) and stop at the gate.

## Rationale

Agent throughput is valuable precisely because it is high, and high throughput applied to irreversible actions multiplies the blast radius of a single misunderstanding. Gates put the human where the human is irreplaceable (judgment, accountability) and nowhere else. The discipline cuts both ways: agents must bring gate-ready proposals (a recommendation, not a menu), and maintainers must actually service the gates rather than rubber-stamping, or the gate is theater.

## What breaks without it

An agent with merge rights eventually merges its own misreading of intent, and the error compounds silently under later work built on top of it. Law amended without sign-off stops being law: the maintainer no longer recognizes the rules agents cite back. Trust erodes in both directions, and the maintainer starts re-checking everything, which costs more than the gates did.
