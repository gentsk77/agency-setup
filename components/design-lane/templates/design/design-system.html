How this sheet works

One section per component or foundation, each a boxed demo with a one-paragraph annotation stating the law it carries. Foundations first (Color, Voice, Type, Shape), then the state grammar, then components. One home per component: extend a section in place rather than adding a duplicate; a section that gets superseded folds into its successor in the same change. Every demo must render correctly in both themes (the toggle above). Conformance against this sheet means computed styles and interaction, not vibes.

Voice & microcopy

This section is law for every string the product ships. Sheet prose and internal docs stay normal writing; user-authored and agent-authored content is not app copy and is out of scope. Two starter rules below are near-universal; add the project's own as they get ruled, and keep the list short enough to actually check against.

  • Say less. Helper text only when the affordance cannot speak for itself; default none.
  • Calm register. Sentence case; no all-caps labels; no urgency theatrics; state the thing, do not perform it.
